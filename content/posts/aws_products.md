AWS is a collection of products and sometimes they have confusing names. Amazon S3 is just short for Simple Storage Solution. Sometimes you can guess what a product is by it's name and other times they are more like code names and they don't give you any clues to what they actually do. Nomenclature is hard.

If you are going to take any AWS exams you'll need to at least be familiar with the every changing catalogue of products. Here is a slide deck of [AWS Products](/awsproducts) that are updated weekly. If you are unfamiliar what they do then click the links to find out more information about each service.
